Privacy Policy

隐私政策

Effective date: October 7, 2026 · Last updated: October 12, 2026

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 7 日 · 最后更新：2026 年 10 月 12 日

In short: your notes stay on your device

一句话总结：你的笔记留在你的设备上

Jotlet has no account system, no server and no cloud sync. There is no analytics SDK, no advertising identifier, and no crash reporting that leaves your phone. Your notes live in the app's local sandbox, and features such as Related Thoughts are computed on device. The only data that leaves your device relates to purchases: to process in-app purchases and unlock Pro, our payment infrastructure provider RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user ID and your purchase history. This purchase data is linked to your identity for the sole purpose of managing your subscription and entitlement, and is never used for tracking or advertising.

Jotlet 没有账号体系、没有服务器、也没有云同步。应用里没有统计 SDK、没有广告标识符，也没有会把数据传出手机的崩溃上报。你的笔记保存在本机的应用沙盒里，「相关想法」这类功能在端侧计算。唯一会离开你设备的数据与购买有关：为处理内购并解锁专业版，我们的支付基础设施服务商 RevenueCat 会收到一个匿名应用用户 ID 及你的购买记录。该购买数据会与你的身份关联，但仅用于管理订阅与权益，绝不用于追踪或广告。

1. What we collect

1. 我们收集什么

Jotlet does not require you to create an account. However, to process in-app purchases and manage your subscription and entitlement, certain purchase-related data is collected by our payment infrastructure provider, RevenueCat. Here is the complete list:

Jotlet 不需要你创建账号。但是，为处理内购并管理订阅与权益，我们的支付基础设施服务商 RevenueCat 会收集以下购买相关数据。完整清单如下：

  • No account, no sign‑up. The App has no login. There is no name, email address, phone number, or advertising identifier required from you.
  • 没有账号，无需注册。本应用没有登录流程。不需要你提供姓名、邮箱地址、电话号码或广告标识符。
  • Purchase data (collected, linked to identity). To unlock Pro and restore purchases, RevenueCat receives an anonymous app-user ID generated on your device and your purchase history (for example, that you own Jotlet Pro). This data is linked to your identity — RevenueCat uses the anonymous app-user ID together with your device identifier to recognize your subscription state across reinstalls — but it is used only for app functionality (managing your subscription and entitlement) and is never used for tracking, profiling or advertising.
  • 购买数据（已收集，与身份关联）。为解锁专业版及支持恢复购买，RevenueCat 会收到一个在你设备上生成的匿名应用用户 ID，以及你的购买记录（例如你拥有 Jotlet 专业版）。这些数据与你的身份关联——RevenueCat 使用匿名应用用户 ID 与你的设备标识符来识别你在重装后的订阅状态——但仅用于App 功能（管理订阅与权益），绝不用于追踪、画像或广告。
  • No analytics SDK. The App contains no third‑party analytics, attribution or usage‑reporting SDK.
  • 没有统计 SDK。本应用不含任何第三方统计分析、归因或使用上报 SDK。
  • No advertising identifiers. We do not request, read or transmit an IDFA or any advertising identifier, and we do not show ads.
  • 没有广告标识符。我们不读取、不请求、不传输 IDFA 或任何广告标识符，也不投放任何广告。
  • No crash reporting off the device. The App does not include crash‑reporting SDKs. When you report a problem by email, we only receive what you choose to write or attach.
  • 没有把数据传出手机的崩溃上报。本应用不含崩溃上报 SDK。你通过邮件反馈问题时，我们只会收到你主动写下的文字与附件。
  • No selling or sharing. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's purposes.
  • 不出售、不共享。我们不出售你的数据，也不会把它共享给他人使用。

2. Where your notes are stored

2. 你的笔记存在哪里

  • Local sandbox only. Every note, tag, attached photo and transcription is written to the App's private storage area on your iPhone. Jotlet operates no server for this content, and we cannot read it.
  • 只在本地沙盒。你的每一条笔记、标签、附图与转写结果，都写入本应用在 iPhone 上的专属存储区域。Jotlet 没有为这些内容运营任何服务器，我们也无法读取它们。
  • No cloud sync. Jotlet does not sync your notes between devices. If you want a copy on another device, use the export feature described in Section 6 and keep the file yourself.
  • 没有云同步。Jotlet 不会在多台设备之间同步你的笔记。如果你希望在另一台设备上有一份副本，请使用第 6 节的导出功能，并自行保管该文件。
  • Device backups are Apple's. If you have enabled iCloud Backup on your iPhone, that device backup is created and managed by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy. It is not operated by us.
  • 设备备份由 Apple 管理。若你在 iPhone 上开启了 iCloud 备份，该设备备份由 Apple 依其自身隐私政策创建与管理，并非由我们运营。
  • You control deletion. Uninstalling the App removes its local data. There is no copy on our side that could be restored, and no way for us to recover it for you.
  • 删除权在你手上。卸载本应用会移除其本地数据。我们这一侧没有任何副本，因此无法为你恢复，也无法代为找回。

3. On‑device intelligence

3. 端侧智能

Some Pro features look "smart" — for example Related Thoughts, which surfaces ideas related to what you just wrote, and tag suggestions. These are computed entirely on your device using Apple's NaturalLanguage framework, together with reading the notes already in your local sandbox.

部分专业版功能看起来很「智能」——例如「相关想法」，它会找出与你刚写的内容相近的想法；还有标签推荐。这些计算完全在你的设备上完成，使用的是 Apple 的 NaturalLanguage（自然语言处理）框架，结合读取本地沙盒中已有的笔记。

  • Your text is never uploaded for analysis, and never leaves the device as part of this computation.
  • 你的文字从不上传去分析，也绝不因这项计算而离开设备。
  • We do not receive the results of Related Thoughts, and we cannot see which notes you have or which suggestions appeared.
  • 我们不会收到「相关想法」的计算结果，也无法知道你有哪些笔记、出现过哪些推荐。
  • Apple provides the NaturalLanguage framework as a system framework; this computation is not performed on Apple's servers.
  • NaturalLanguage 是 Apple 提供的系统框架，这项计算并非在 Apple 的服务器上执行。

Voice notes are transcribed on device so that the text lands in the input box and stays editable before you save. The audio is not retained as a separate voice note after saving, and it is not uploaded.

语音笔记在端侧转写，转写出的文字会填进输入框，保存前可自由编辑。保存后音频不会作为独立的语音笔记保留，也不上传。

4. Permissions

4. 权限

Jotlet asks for a permission only at the moment you actually use the feature that needs it. It never asks for camera or microphone access at launch.

Jotlet 只在你真正使用某个功能的那一刻，才申请该功能所需的权限。应用启动时绝不会索要相机或麦克风权限。

Microphone

麦克风

Requested only when you tap the Voice button, to record the note you are about to write.

仅在你点击「语音」按钮、准备录制当下这条笔记时申请。

Camera

相机

Requested only when you tap the Photo button, to take the picture you want to attach.

仅在你点击「图片」按钮、准备拍摄要附上的照片时申请。

  • No photo library access. Jotlet does not request permission to read your system photo library, and it never browses, reads or uploads your existing photos. Photos enter the App only through the in‑app camera you trigger yourself.
  • 不读取系统相册。Jotlet 不申请读取系统相册的权限，也绝不浏览、读取或上传你已有的照片。照片只能通过你自己在应用内触发的相机进入本应用。
  • No location, contacts, or tracking permission. The App does not request location, contacts, Bluetooth, calendar or health access.
  • 不申请位置、通讯录等权限。本应用不申请位置、通讯录、蓝牙、日历或健康数据的访问权限。
  • Declining is fine. If you refuse a permission, the rest of the App still works; only the corresponding input mode is unavailable.
  • 拒绝也没关系。如果你拒绝某项权限，应用的其他部分照常可用，只是对应的输入方式无法使用。

5. Purchases

5. 购买

  • Apple handles all payment. Pro is purchased through the Apple App Store. Payment is processed by Apple; Jotlet has no access to your card number, bank details or billing address.
  • 支付完全由 Apple 处理。专业版通过 Apple App Store 购买。支付由 Apple 处理，Jotlet 无法接触你的卡号、银行信息或账单地址。
  • Purchase processing. Jotlet uses Apple's standard in‑app purchase API. To unlock Pro and restore purchases, the App works with our payment infrastructure provider, RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app‑user ID generated on your device and your purchase history (for example, that you own Jotlet Pro). This data is linked to your identity — RevenueCat uses the anonymous app-user ID together with your device identifier to recognize your subscription state — but it is used only for app functionality (managing your subscription and entitlement), and is never used for tracking, profiling or advertising. We never receive your Apple ID, name, email or payment details.
  • 购买处理。Jotlet 使用 Apple 的标准内购接口。为解锁专业版及支持恢复购买，应用会与我们的支付基础设施服务商 RevenueCat 协作。RevenueCat 会收到一个在你设备上生成的匿名应用用户 ID，以及你的购买记录（例如你拥有 Jotlet 专业版）。这些数据与你的身份关联——RevenueCat 使用匿名应用用户 ID 与你的设备标识符来识别你的订阅状态——但仅用于App 功能（管理订阅与权益），绝不用于追踪、画像或广告。我们也绝不会收到你的 Apple ID、姓名、邮箱或支付信息。
  • No tracking, no advertising. Purchase data handled through RevenueCat is used only to unlock and restore Pro. It is never used for tracking, profiling or advertising, and it is not sold or shared for anyone else's purposes.
  • 不追踪、不用于广告。经由 RevenueCat 处理的购买数据仅用于解锁与恢复专业版权益，绝不用于追踪、画像或广告，也不会被出售或共享给他人使用。
  • Restore Purchases. After changing device or reinstalling, you can restore your Pro entitlement with the "Restore Purchases" button on the purchase page. This queries Apple's records via RevenueCat on your behalf; it is not an account on our side.
  • 恢复购买。换设备或重装后，你可以在购买页点击「恢复购买」找回专业版权益。该操作由应用经由 RevenueCat 向 Apple 的记录发起查询，我们这边并不存在任何账号。
  • No subscription required. The recommended option is a one‑time purchase. Monthly and yearly options are also available and are managed through your Apple account settings.
  • 无需订阅。推荐选项是一次性买断。同时也提供按月与按年的选项，均可在你的 Apple 账户设置中管理。

6. Export and your own backups

6. 导出与你自己保管的备份

  • Export is never locked. The basic export is available on the free plan. There is no gate on your ability to take your notes out.
  • 导出从不设锁。基础导出在免费版即可使用。我们不会用任何方式限制你把自己的笔记取出来。
  • The file belongs to you. When you export, the App writes a file into its own folder on your device and then opens the iOS share sheet. You decide where it goes — including your own iCloud Drive through the Files app — and you can delete it at any time. We never receive it.
  • 文件归你所有。导出时，本应用会在设备上的应用文件夹内生成文件，然后打开 iOS 分享面板。文件去哪里由你决定——包括通过「文件」App 存进你自己的 iCloud 云盘——你也可以随时删除它。我们不会收到它。
  • Backups are your responsibility. Because we hold no copy, keeping a regular export is the only way to protect your notes if the App is uninstalled or the device is lost.
  • 备份由你负责。由于我们不持有任何副本，只有定期导出，才能在应用被卸载或设备丢失时保护你的笔记。

7. Your rights and choices

7. 你的权利与选择

Because Jotlet has no account system and no server, your notes are always under your own control on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them. You may also contact us using the address in Section 9 with any question, and we will respond within the time required by applicable law.

由于 Jotlet 既没有账号体系也没有服务器，你的笔记始终由你在设备上自行掌控。卸载本应用即会将其删除。如有任何疑问，你也可以通过第 9 节的邮箱联系我们，我们会在适用法律要求的期限内回应。

8. Children's privacy

8. 儿童隐私

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has used the App, all local content can be removed simply by uninstalling it.

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，我们也不会在知情的情况下收集儿童的个人信息。如家长或监护人发现儿童使用了本应用，只需卸载应用即可删除全部本地内容。
