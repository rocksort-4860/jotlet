Quick notes, nothing else

只做速记，别的都不管

Two seconds to get the thought out.

两秒把想法拿出来

Jotlet is a note app that only records. It never asks you to check in, never counts streaks, never nags you. Two tabs — Capture to write, Organize to read — and everything stays on your device.

Jotlet 是一款只做记录的速记 App。它不拿进度压你、不统计连续天数、也不会写小作文催你回来。只有两个标签页——快记录负责写，慢整理负责看——而且所有内容都留在你自己的设备上。

Captured in seconds

两秒就记完

One big input field sits right above the keyboard, with four equal buttons: Text, Voice, Photo, Save. After saving, the keyboard stays up and the cursor stays in the box, so you can keep going.

输入框很大，紧贴键盘上方，是四个等宽按钮：文字、语音、图片、保存。保存之后键盘不收起、光标还留在输入框里，可以接着记第二三条。

Text, voice, photos

文字、语音、图片

Three ways in, one Save button. Voice is transcribed into the text box so you can edit it before saving. Photos show as a thumbnail strip above the input, with a short note attached.

三种输入，最后都汇聚到同一个「保存」按钮。语音录完自动转成文字填进输入框，可以编辑后再保存；照片以缩略条显示在输入框上方，附一段文字注释。

Search, filter, export

搜索、筛选、导出

Search, filter and basic export are free and fully open — they used to be Pro-only. Your notes are listed newest first, with no folder tree to maintain.

搜索、筛选与基础导出现已免费全开放（此前是专业版专有）。笔记按时间倒序排列，没有需要维护的文件夹树。

On-device, zero upload

端侧计算，零上传

No account, no server, no cloud sync. Related Thoughts and tag suggestions are computed on your phone with Apple's NaturalLanguage framework. Your words are never uploaded for analysis.

没有账号、没有服务器、没有云同步。「相关想法」和标签推荐都在手机端用 Apple NaturalLanguage 框架计算，你的文字从不上传去分析。

Our Philosophy

我们的理念

Less nagging, more writing

少一点催促，多一点记录

Most note apps turn into projects. Jotlet stays a notepad: open it, put the thought down, close it. There is no daily goal to fail at, no streak to protect, no score to keep up. If a tool makes you write less, it is the wrong tool.

大多数速记 App 最后都会变成一个「项目」。Jotlet 坚持只当一个记事本：打开，把想法写下来，关掉。没有必须完成的目标，没有要保住的连续记录，没有要赶上的分数。如果一个工具让你写得越来越少，那它就是错的工具。

Free forever — the core never moves behind a paywall

永久免费——核心记录功能永不进付费墙

The free plan is a real app, not a demo. Unlock more later with a one-time purchase whenever you feel like it.

免费版是一个真正能用的应用，不是残缺的演示版。想解锁更多时，随时可以一次性买断。
