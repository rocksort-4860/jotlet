Quick notes, nothing else

只做速记，别的都不管

Two seconds to get the thought out.
两秒把想法拿出来

Jotlet is a note‑taking app focused purely on fast capture. It never pushes check‑ins, streaks or gamification. Two simple tabs: Capture for writing, Organize for reviewing. All notes stay locally on your device.

Jotlet 是一款专注快速记录的笔记App。没有打卡、没有连续记录、没有游戏化激励。仅两个标签页：快记录用来写，慢整理用来查阅。全部笔记保存在本机设备。

Captured in seconds

两秒就记完

One large input sits right above keyboard, four straightforward buttons: Text, Voice, Photo, Save. After saving, keyboard remains open and cursor stays in‑place so you can keep writing successive thoughts quickly.

超大输入框紧贴键盘上方，四个简洁按钮：文字、语音、图片、保存。保存后键盘不收起，光标保持在位，可以快速连续记录多条想法。

Text, voice, photos

文字、语音、图片

Three input ways converge to single Save button. Voice gets transcribed‑to‑text for editing before saving. Photos appear as thumbnail strips attached to short written notes.

三种输入方式统一到同一个保存按钮。语音转文字，保存前可以编辑；照片以缩略条形式附加到简短文字笔记上。

Search, filter, export

搜索、筛选、导出

Search, filtering and basic export features are fully free. Notes list ordered newest‑first, without complex folder structures you need to manage manually.

搜索、筛选、基础导出全部免费开放。笔记按时间倒序列出，不需要维护复杂文件夹体系。

On‑device, zero upload

端侧计算，零上传

No account, no backend server, no cloud sync. Smart features like Related Thoughts and tag suggestions run locally via Apple NaturalLanguage. Your written content is never uploaded externally for analysis.

无账号、无后端服务器、无云同步。相关想法、标签推荐等智能能力完全本地运行，使用Apple NaturalLanguage框架。你的文字内容永远不会上传到外部进行分析。

Our Philosophy
我们的理念

Less nagging, more writing

少一点催促，多一点记录

Many note‑taking apps turn into heavy projects. Jotlet stays minimal: open, jot a thought, close. There are no daily goals you can fail, no streaks you must preserve. If a tool discourages you from writing, it is not suitable.

很多笔记App最后变成沉重负担。Jotlet保持极简：打开，记下想法，关闭。不存在会失败的每日目标，也没有需要守护的连续记录。如果工具反而降低你的记录意愿，那就是不合适的工具。

Free forever — core functions will never sit behind paywall

永久免费——核心记录功能永不设置付费墙

Free tier delivers fully usable real‑world app experience instead of limited demo. Unlock advanced features later with one‑time purchase whenever you want.

免费版提供完整可用的实际产品体验，不是残缺演示版。需要时可以一次性买断解锁高级能力。
