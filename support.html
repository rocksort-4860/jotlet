Frequently asked questions

常见问题

Q: Where are my notes stored, and can they be lost?

Q：我的笔记存在哪里？会不会丢？

Every note is written to Jotlet's private storage area on your own iPhone. Nothing is uploaded and there is no cloud copy, so there is no server that could fail. Uninstalling the App, or clearing local storage, does delete them permanently — we hold no backup and cannot restore them for you. The safe habit is to run the free export from Settings now and then and keep the file somewhere you control.

每一条笔记都写入本应用在你 iPhone 上的专属存储区域。内容不会上传，也没有云端副本，所以不存在会故障的服务器。卸载应用或清除本地存储确实会永久删除它们——我们没有任何备份，也无法为你恢复。稳妥的做法是偶尔在设置页用免费导出生成文件，存到你自己掌控的地方。

Q: Why is there a limit of 30 notes?

Q：为什么有 30 条的上限？

The free plan keeps 30 notes on your device. The count is about how many notes you are storing right now, not how many you have ever written: delete one and a slot opens up immediately. Save a hundred over a month and delete the fifty you no longer need, and you never hit the wall. Pro removes the cap entirely if you would rather keep everything.

免费版在你的设备上保留 30 条笔记。这个上限算的是「当前存着多少条」，不是你累计写过多少条：删掉一条，立刻就腾出一个位置。一个月里写了上百条、把不需要的五十条删掉，你就不会撞到上限。如果你更想全部留着，专业版可以彻底解除这个限制。

Q: Do my notes sync between devices?

Q：我的笔记会在多台设备之间同步吗？

No — Jotlet has no account system and no cloud sync, so notes never travel between your iPhone, your iPad or a new device. If you want a copy elsewhere, use export and keep the resulting file yourself (your own iCloud Drive through the Files app is a fine place). Import it back on the other device when you need it.

不会。Jotlet 既没有账号体系也没有云同步，因此笔记不会在你的 iPhone、iPad 或新设备之间自动流转。若想在别处留一份，请使用导出功能，并自行保管生成的文件（通过「文件」App 存进你自己的 iCloud 云盘就很合适）。需要时再把它导入到另一台设备。

Q: After changing phones or reinstalling, is my Pro purchase still there?

Q：换手机或重装后，买断的专业版还在吗？

Yes. Open the purchase page in Jotlet and tap Restore Purchases, which sits on the same page as the three prices. Sign in with the Apple ID that made the purchase and your entitlement comes back — you do not pay twice. Your purchase record lives on Apple's servers, so losing the App from a device does not lose the purchase.

在。打开 Jotlet 的购买页，点击「恢复购买」，该入口与三个价格在同一个页面上。用当初购买时的 Apple ID 登录，权益就会回来，不需要付第二次钱。你的购买记录保存在 Apple 的服务器上，所以某台设备上删掉了应用，购买本身不会消失。

Q: Can I get a refund?

Q：可以退款吗？

Payments are handled by Apple, so refunds follow Apple policy and must be requested through Apple rather than through us — you can use reportaproblem.apple.com or the App Store on your device. We cannot issue refunds directly. If a purchase failed or Pro never unlocked, tap Restore Purchases first; if it still does not work, email 365504860@qq.com and we will help.

支付由 Apple 处理，因此退款按 Apple 政策执行，且需要通过 Apple 申请而非由我们受理——你可以使用 reportaproblem.apple.com 或设备上的 App Store。我们无法直接办理退款。若购买未成功或专业版没有解锁，请先点击「恢复购买」；若仍有问题，可邮件联系 365504860@qq.com，我们会协助处理。

Q: Will you use my data for ads?

Q：你们会拿我的数据做广告吗？

No. Jotlet shows no ads, contains no advertising or analytics SDK, and we do not sell your data to anyone. Your notes never leave your device. The only thing that reaches Apple's servers is the purchase transaction itself, which we cannot see. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

不会。Jotlet 不投放广告，不含广告或统计 SDK，我们也不会把你的数据卖给任何人。你的笔记从不离开你的设备。唯一会到达 Apple 服务器的是购买交易本身，而我们看不到它。完整细节见《隐私政策》。

Q: What do the Voice and Photo buttons actually do?

Q：「语音」和「图片」按钮到底做了什么？

All three inputs end up at the same Save button. Voice is transcribed into the text box so you can edit the words before saving — it does not become a separate audio note. A photo appears as a thumbnail strip above the input box; add a short note and save. Camera and microphone are requested only when you tap those buttons, never at launch, and the App does not read your system photo library.

三种输入最后都汇聚到同一个「保存」按钮。语音会转写成文字填进输入框，保存前可以随意修改，不会变成一条独立的语音笔记。照片会以缩略条显示在输入框上方，附一段文字注释后保存。相机与麦克风权限只在你点击对应按钮时申请，启动时绝不索要；本应用也不会读取你的系统相册。
